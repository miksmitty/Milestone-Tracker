Gantt chart

Open a programme to see its Gantt chart, items and reports

Each programme has its own items and reports. Use Edit to rename a programme, set its RAG, dates and people, or choose what it calls its items — for example Deliverables and Work packages instead of milestones and tasks. Programmes are stored in programs.csv; items and reports carry the program_id they belong to.

Changes save automatically

Quick update shows the fields that change most — ref, title, type, owner, RAG and dates: click a RAG to change it, or edit any cell. Switch to All fields for lanes, links, shape and the rest. Click a column header to sort, use the filter row to narrow the list, and drag a header edge to resize (double-click to reset). ID is the primary key: assigned automatically, never changes or gets reused, and is what links between items point at. Ref is your own label (e.g. 4.1). Data is stored in milestones.csv next to the server — edit it in a spreadsheet if you prefer, then hit Reload. Type says whether something is a milestone (one date, drawn as a shape: changing either date moves it) or a task (a start and end, drawn as a bar). Change the type to give a milestone a date range, or to collapse a task to one date. Changing an end date shifts everything that depends on it by the same amount.

Click a report to view or edit it. Sort, filter and resize columns as on the Items tab. To report on something, click it on the Gantt chart and choose Provide report, or use Report… on the Items tab. Reports are stored in reports.csv, one row per report, linked to what it covers by its ID.
