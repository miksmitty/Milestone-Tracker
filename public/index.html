Risks are what might happen, issues what has happened, dependencies what the plan needs from others or they need from it. Each can be linked to any number of items and streams: click a link to see it on the plan, or a row to edit the entry.

A risk’s rating is likelihood × impact (6 or more out of 9 is High), an issue’s its impact and a dependency’s its priority. A due date in red has passed while the entry is still open. Status updates keep their date and time and are never edited.

Set the statuses, likelihood and impact levels, priorities and dependency types the register offers in Workspace settings → Register.

Quick update shows the fields that change most — ref, title, type, owner, RAG and dates: click a RAG to change it, or edit any cell. All fields adds streams, links and the rest.

Tick rows (shift-click for a range, or the header box for all) to change a field on many at once, move their dates or delete them. Headings work like a spreadsheet’s: ▾ sorts or filters, drag a heading to move the column or its edge to resize it (double-click to fit), right-click for autofit.

Type: a milestone has one date and is drawn as a diamond; a task has a start and end and is drawn as a bar; a top level milestone is under change control. Changing an end date shifts everything that depends on it. If you edit the data in a spreadsheet, Reload picks it up.

Weekly updates are the weekly RAG, trend and summary for the workspace as a whole and for each stream: what the Portfolio and Streams tables show. They’re listed here week by week, newest first; click one to read or edit it.

+ New update records this week’s update for the workspace. A stream’s update is recorded from its row on Streams, or by clicking its last one here and changing the week.

Due lists what still needs a report this period, by owner: anything that has started or already has reports, until it reaches a done status. Each follows the cadence of its latest report (weekly if it has none); a report whose period reaches into the current one covers it. Periods missed counts the periods since its last report.
